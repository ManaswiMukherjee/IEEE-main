PURPOSE:
Convert the machine-readable city plan produced by Subsystem 3
into a human-understandable 3D representation of the proposed
city.

INPUT:
CITY PLAN from Subsystem 3

Example:

{
    "roads": [...],
    "buildings": [...],
    "parks": [...],
    "solar_farms": [...],
    "wind_farms": [...],
    "water_infrastructure": [...]
}

PROCESS:
Interpret each city-plan element and generate its corresponding
3D representation.

Examples:

CITY PLAN ELEMENT          → 3D REPRESENTATION

Road                       → 3D road
Residential building       → 3D building
Commercial zone            → Building cluster
Park                       → Vegetation / terrain
Solar farm                 → Solar panels
Wind farm                  → Wind turbines
Water body                 → Water surface
Public transport           → Roads / stations / vehicles

The system should preserve the locations and relationships
defined by the city planner.

OUTPUT:
Interactive 3D representation of the proposed city.

Possible features:
- Camera navigation
- Zoom / rotation
- Different city layers
- Renewable infrastructure visualization
- Energy infrastructure visualization
- Sustainability statistics
- Before/after comparison, if applicable

TYPE:
Procedural 3D generation / visualization

IMPORTANT:
This subsystem does NOT decide where things should go.

It visualizes the plan generated by Subsystem 3.

CITY PLAN
    ↓
PROCEDURAL 3D GENERATION
    ↓
INTERACTIVE 3D CITY
