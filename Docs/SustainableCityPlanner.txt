PURPOSE:
Generate a machine-readable plan for how a city should be
organized within a given geographic area while satisfying the
user's requirements and maximizing sustainability.

INPUT:
1. Structured requirements from Subsystem 2
2. Geographic information
    - Terrain
    - Elevation
    - Land availability
    - Water bodies
    - Existing roads
    - Existing infrastructure
    - Restricted/protected areas

3. Environmental information
    - Solar potential
    - Wind potential
    - Climate
    - Temperature
    - Rainfall
    - Other relevant environmental factors

4. Population / development requirements

5. Renewable-energy requirements

PROCESS:
Generate and evaluate possible city layouts while respecting
physical and logical constraints.

The planner should determine suitable locations for things such as:

- Residential areas
- Commercial areas
- Industrial areas
- Roads
- Public transportation
- Parks / green areas
- Water infrastructure
- Solar installations
- Wind installations
- Other required infrastructure

The planner should optimize for objectives such as:

- Renewable-energy utilization
- Low grid dependency
- Reduced transportation distance
- Walkability
- Green-space availability
- Infrastructure efficiency
- Environmental impact
- Population capacity
- Geographic suitability

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
Examples:
- Buildings cannot overlap
- Infrastructure cannot be placed in restricted areas
- Roads must remain connected
- Population capacity must be sufficient
- Required infrastructure must be reachable
- Renewable infrastructure must be placed in suitable locations

OUTPUT:
A standardized machine-readable CITY PLAN.

Example:

{
    "buildings": [...],
    "roads": [...],
    "residential_zones": [...],
    "commercial_zones": [...],
    "parks": [...],
    "solar_farms": [...],
    "wind_farms": [...],
    "water_infrastructure": [...],
    "public_transport": [...]
}

TYPE:
Optimization / procedural planning / potentially ML-assisted

IMPORTANT:
This subsystem should NOT directly generate a 3D scene.

Its job is to produce the logical structure of the city.
